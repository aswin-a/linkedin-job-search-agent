# Search Profile (template)

Used by the **job-search** skill to search LinkedIn and score jobs. Copy into a Google Doc, fill it in, and add it to Project Knowledge.

- Target titles (priority order): e.g. Senior Product Manager - Platform; Platform Product Manager; Senior PM (APIs / Integrations); Senior PM - AI
- Also consider: e.g. Lead Product Manager; Group Product Manager
- Exclude titles containing: e.g. Associate, Intern, Project Manager, Sales, Marketing

## Fit score weights (0–100)
Scored against the Master Resume and the full JD. Score each factor 0–100, multiply by its weight, add up.

| Factor | Weight | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Must-have skills and tools | 35% | Share of the JD's must-have skills/tools/methods the Master Resume genuinely shows |
| Relevant experience and responsibilities | 25% | How closely past responsibilities match the day-to-day of this role |
| Evidence of results | 15% | Quantified outcomes that prove the JD's key responsibilities |
| Domain and product context | 15% | Industry, product type and customer type vs background |
| Seniority and scope of ownership | 10% | Title level, years asked, team size, decision ownership |

Not scored, used as filters: location and work mode, posting date, excluded titles and companies. Recency, applicant count and company preference go in tracker Notes only.
